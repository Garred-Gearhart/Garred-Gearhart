## About the evidence

These are educational projects, not client engagements or production SOC employment. Each write-up distinguishes the work I performed from response actions I recommended. Evidence tables summarize my original reports and notes. Raw datasets, original screenshots, credentials, and course worksheets are not bundled here.

The included SPL files are adapted from my investigation notes. They document my approach and require the original dataset and field mappings to reproduce; they have not been rerun as part of preparing this portfolio.
